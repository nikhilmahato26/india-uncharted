2 Nights 3 Days Golden City Jaisalmer Tour Package

If you’re planning a short escape into Rajasthan’s desert charm, this 2 Nights 3 Days Golden City Jaisalmer Tour gives you a clean, well-paced experience without rushing through the highlights. It covers the city’s heritage and the iconic desert stay without unnecessary fillers.

Tour Overview

  • Duration: 2 Nights / 3 Days

  • Pickup & Drop: Jaisalmer Railway Station / Airport
  • Tour Type: Private / On Demand
  • Ideal For: Couples, families, small groups 

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Jaisalmer – Fort & City Exploration

After arrival in Jaisalmer, you’ll be picked up and transferred to your hotel. Once settled, start exploring the city’s core attractions:

  • Visit the iconic Jaisalmer Fort – one of the few living forts in the world
  • Explore the detailed architecture of Patwon Ki Haveli
  • Visit the peaceful Gadisar Lake for sunset views
  • Explore the ancient Jain Temples in Jaisalmer inside the fort

Return to the hotel for dinner and overnight stay.

Day 2: Desert Experience at Sam Sand Dunes

After breakfast, check out and head towards the desert at Sam Sand Dunes.

  • Enjoy a thrilling camel safari across the dunes
  • An optional jeep safari for a more adventurous ride
  • Witness a stunning desert sunset
  • Check in at a luxury desert camp

In the evening:

  • Enjoy Rajasthani folk dance & music
  • Dinner with a bonfire under the open sky

Overnight stay in a desert camp.

Day 3: Departure from Jaisalmer

Wake up to a peaceful desert morning. After breakfast, check out from the camp and transfer back to Jaisalmer for your departure.
