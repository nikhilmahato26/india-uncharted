Why Visit Pushkar?

Located near Ajmer in Rajasthan, Pushkar is one of the oldest and holiest cities in India. The town is best known for the sacred Pushkar Lake, surrounded by beautiful ghats and temples where pilgrims and travelers gather for prayers, rituals, and peaceful moments by the water.

Pushkar is also home to the famous Brahma Temple, one of the very few temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma. The spiritual atmosphere, evening aarti ceremonies, and temple bells create a peaceful and unforgettable experience for visitors.

Apart from spirituality, Pushkar is known for its lively markets, rooftop cafés, traditional handicrafts, folk music, and desert culture. Travelers can enjoy camel safaris, jeep rides, village tours, hot air balloon rides, and cultural performances in the nearby desert landscapes.

The town becomes especially vibrant during the famous Pushkar Camel Fair, attracting visitors from around the world with camel trading, cultural events, competitions, folk dances, and colorful Rajasthani traditions.
