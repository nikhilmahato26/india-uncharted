Best Time to Travel

Goa is a year-round destination, but the ideal period for a yoga and wellness holiday is:

October to March

  • Pleasant temperatures between 22°C and 32°C
  • Sunny days and cool evenings
  • Perfect weather for yoga, meditation, beach walks, and outdoor activities
  • Peak season with a vibrant atmosphere and cultural events

April to May

  • Warm and sunny weather
  • Fewer crowds and attractive prices
  • Ideal for travelers seeking a quieter wellness experience

June to September (Monsoon Season)

  • Lush green landscapes and refreshing rainfall
  • Peaceful atmosphere for relaxation and introspection
  • Excellent time for wellness, Ayurveda, meditation, and digital detox retreats
