Tour Itinerary

🕌 1. Jama Masjid Exploration

Start your journey at:

  • Jama Masjid

Highlights:

  • One of India’s largest mosques
  • Stunning Mughal architecture
  • Panoramic views of Old Delhi
  • Historic Islamic heritage

Learn fascinating stories about Mughal Delhi and Shah Jahan’s era.

🚶 2. Chandni Chowk Heritage Walk

Walk through the famous:

  • Chandni Chowk Market

Experience:

  • Narrow historic lanes
  • Traditional spice markets
  • Jewelry & textile shops
  • Old havelis & hidden courtyards

Feel the vibrant energy of one of India’s oldest markets.

🌶️ 3. Spice Market Experience

Visit the famous spice market of Old Delhi.

Discover:

  • Exotic Indian spices
  • Traditional trading culture
  • Aromatic local products
  • Busy wholesale market atmosphere

Perfect for photography and cultural exploration.

🍛 4. Delhi Street Food Tasting

Taste authentic Old Delhi flavors including:

  • Paratha
  • Chaat
  • Jalebi
  • Kebabs
  • Masala chai

Experience Delhi’s legendary street food culture.

🚲 5. Rickshaw Ride Through Old Delhi

Enjoy a traditional cycle rickshaw ride through:

  • Historic bazaars
  • Hidden alleys
  • Local residential areas

A fun and authentic way to explore Old Delhi.

🏛️ 6. Red Fort Exterior View

End your tour near:

  • Red Fort

Learn about:

  • Mughal imperial history
  • Indian independence stories
  • Delhi’s transformation over centuries
