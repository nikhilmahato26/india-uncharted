Rajasthan Wildlife Tour Package (6 Days / 5 Nights)

Experience the wild side of Rajasthan with this carefully planned wildlife tour covering famous national parks, bird sanctuaries, and jungle safaris. This package is ideal for travelers who want nature, photography, and adventure alongside Rajasthan’s royal atmosphere.

Tour Overview

Duration: 5 Nights / 6 Days
Destinations Covered: Ranthambore – Bharatpur – Sariska
Pickup & Drop: Jaipur Airport / Railway Station
Tour Type: Private Wildlife Tour
Ideal For: Wildlife lovers, photographers, families, couples

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Jaipur – Transfer to Ranthambore

After arrival in Jaipur, drive towards Ranthambore National Park.

  • Check-in at wildlife resort
  • Evening leisure time
  • Optional nature walk
  • Dinner & overnight stay

Day 2: Ranthambore Jungle Safari

Start early morning for an exciting jungle safari.

Highlights:

  • Chance to spot the Royal Bengal Tiger
  • Leopards, deer, crocodiles & exotic birds
  • Explore ancient ruins inside the forest

Afternoon:

  • Relax at resort
  • Evening safari (optional)

Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 3: Ranthambore to Bharatpur

After breakfast, drive to Keoladeo National Park.

  • Check-in at hotel
  • Evening birdwatching experience
  • Optional cycle rickshaw ride inside sanctuary

Overnight stay in Bharatpur.

Day 4: Bharatpur to Sariska

Morning bird sanctuary visit followed by drive to Sariska Tiger Reserve.

  • Check-in at jungle resort
  • Leisure evening amidst nature
  • Bonfire & dinner

Day 5: Sariska Safari & Nature Experience

Enjoy morning safari in Sariska Tiger Reserve.

Wildlife includes:

  • Tigers
  • Hyenas
  • Jackals
  • Sambhar deer
  • Rare bird species

Afternoon free for relaxation or village exploration.

Dinner & overnight stay.

Day 6: Departure to Jaipur

After breakfast, drive back to Jaipur for departure.
