Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Srinagar – Romantic Houseboat Stay

Welcome to Srinagar.

After airport pickup:

  • Transfer to luxury houseboat on Dal Lake
  • Welcome drink & honeymoon room setup

Evening:

  • Private Shikara ride on Dal Lake
  • Enjoy sunset lake views & floating markets

Romantic dinner & overnight stay in houseboat.

Day 2: Srinagar Local Sightseeing

Explore Srinagar’s beautiful gardens and lakes.

Visit:

  • Mughal Gardens
  • Nishat Garden
  • Shalimar Garden
  • Chashme Shahi

Optional:

  • Kashmiri traditional dress photography
  • Local handicraft shopping

Overnight stay in Srinagar hotel.

Day 3: Gulmarg Snow Romance

Drive to Gulmarg.

Activities:

  • Gondola cable car ride (optional)
  • Snow photography
  • Skiing & snow activities during winter

Enjoy breathtaking Himalayan views with your partner.

Romantic overnight stay.

Day 4: Pahalgam Valley Experience

Drive to Pahalgam.

En route:

  • Apple orchards
  • Saffron fields
  • Scenic mountain villages

Visit:

  • Betaab Valley
  • Lidder River viewpoints
  • Aru Valley (optional)

Enjoy peaceful mountain moments together.

Overnight stay.

Day 5: Srinagar Leisure & Romantic Evening

Return to Srinagar.

Relax and enjoy:

  • Café experiences
  • Lakeside walk
  • Romantic candlelight dinner

Optional:

  • Couple photography session

Overnight stay.

Day 6: Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Srinagar Airport for departure.

 

Tour ends with unforgettable honeymoon memories in Kashmir.
