200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course (TTC) in Rishikesh

Deepen your yoga practice and begin your journey as a certified yoga teacher with this 200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course in Rishikesh. Designed for beginners and intermediate practitioners, this immersive program combines traditional yoga teachings, meditation, pranayama, anatomy, philosophy, and practical teaching experience in the peaceful Himalayan surroundings of Rishikesh.

This course offers a balanced experience of learning, self-discovery, mindfulness, and spiritual growth.
