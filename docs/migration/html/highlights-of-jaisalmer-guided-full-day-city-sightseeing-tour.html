                      Detailed Sightseeing Itinerary

 

🏰 1. Jaisalmer Fort Exploration

Begin the tour with the magnificent:

  • Jaisalmer Fort

Highlights:

  • One of the world’s few living forts
  • Ancient Rajput architecture
  • Narrow golden lanes & local shops
  • Stunning city viewpoints

Inside the fort:

  • Jain Temples
  • Royal palace areas
  • Traditional handicraft markets

🕌 2. Patwon Ki Haveli

Visit the famous:

  • Patwon Ki Haveli

Highlights:

  • Intricate sandstone carvings
  • Royal merchant history
  • Heritage photography opportunities
  • Traditional Rajasthani architecture

Perfect place for cultural photography.

🏛️ 3. Nathmal Ki Haveli & Salim Singh Ki Haveli

Explore:

  • Nathmal Ki Haveli
  • Salim Singh Ki Haveli

Learn about:

  • Historic merchant families
  • Unique haveli architecture
  • Artistic craftsmanship of Rajasthan

🌅 4. Gadisar Lake

Visit the peaceful:

  • Gadisar Lake

Activities:

  • Relax by the lakeside
  • Sunset photography
  • Bird watching during winter season
  • Traditional chhatri architecture views

A perfect place to experience calm desert beauty.

🛍️ 5. Local Market & Cultural Walk

Explore local markets for:

  • Handmade textiles
  • Leather goods
  • Rajasthani jewelry
  • Traditional souvenirs

Interact with local artisans and experience desert culture.
