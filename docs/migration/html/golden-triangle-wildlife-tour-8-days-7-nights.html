Golden Triangle Wildlife Tour (8 Days / 7 Nights)

If you want to explore India’s most famous heritage circuit along with a real wildlife experience, this Golden Triangle Wildlife Tour gives you a balanced mix of history, culture, architecture, and jungle safari. The tour covers Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, and Ranthambore National Park without making the itinerary feel rushed or overloaded.

Tour Overview

Duration: 8 Days / 7 Nights
Destinations Covered: Delhi, Agra, Ranthambore & Jaipur
Pickup & Drop: Delhi Airport / Railway Station
Tour Type: Private / On Demand
Ideal For: Couples, families, wildlife lovers, and international travelers

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Delhi

After arrival in Delhi, you’ll be received and transferred to your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, begin exploring some of the capital city’s major attractions.

Visit:

  • India Gate
  • Qutub Minar
  • Humayun’s Tomb
  • Lotus Temple
  • Drive past Parliament House & Rashtrapati Bhavan

Return to the hotel for overnight stay.

Day 2: Delhi Sightseeing

After breakfast, continue exploring Old and New Delhi.

Highlights include:

  • Red Fort (outside view)
  • Jama Masjid
  • Chandni Chowk
  • Raj Ghat
  • Akshardham Temple

In the evening, relax at the hotel and prepare for the next destination.

Day 3: Delhi to Agra

After breakfast, drive to Agra. On arrival, check in at the hotel and begin sightseeing.

Visit:

  • The world-famous Taj Mahal
  • Agra Fort
  • Mehtab Bagh for sunset views of the Taj Mahal

Overnight stay in Agra.

Day 4: Agra to Ranthambore

After breakfast, drive towards Ranthambore. En route, visit Fatehpur Sikri, the historic Mughal city known for its architecture and royal palaces.

Continue the journey to Ranthambore and check in at the wildlife resort.

Dinner and overnight stay at the resort.

Day 5: Ranthambore Wildlife Safari

Early morning jungle safari at Ranthambore National Park, one of the best places in India to spot Royal Bengal Tigers in their natural habitat.

During the safari, you may also spot:

  • Leopards
  • Deer
  • Sloth bears
  • Crocodiles
  • Various bird species

After breakfast and rest at the resort, enjoy an evening safari for another wildlife experience.

Dinner and overnight stay in Ranthambore.

Day 6: Ranthambore to Jaipur

After breakfast, drive to Jaipur, the Pink City of Rajasthan.

On arrival, check in at the hotel and visit:

  • Albert Hall Museum
  • Birla Temple
  • Local Jaipur markets

Overnight stay in Jaipur.

Day 7: Jaipur Sightseeing

After breakfast, explore Jaipur’s major attractions.

Visit:

  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
  • Hawa Mahal
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Jal Mahal

You can also enjoy optional activities like elephant ride or traditional Rajasthani dinner experience.

Return to the hotel for overnight stay.

Day 8: Departure from Jaipur / Delhi

 

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. Depending on your departure plan, you’ll be transferred to Jaipur Airport or driven back to Delhi for your onward journey.
