Rajasthan is not just about forts and palaces—it is also one of India’s most thrilling wildlife destinations. This 10-day Rajasthan Wildlife Photography Tour by India Uncharted is designed for photographers, wildlife enthusiasts, and nature lovers seeking rare and powerful encounters with India’s iconic species.

From the dense forests of Ranthambore National Park to the rugged wilderness of Jawai Leopard Hills, this journey offers unmatched opportunities to photograph tigers, leopards, sloth bears, migratory birds, and more.

Whether you're a professional photographer or a passionate beginner, our expert naturalists and photography guides ensure you return with extraordinary shots and unforgettable experiences.

Tour Overview

Duration: 9 Nights / 10 Days
Destinations Covered: Jaipur – Ranthambore – Bharatpur – Jawai – Jodhpur – Jaisalmer
Pickup & Drop: Jaipur Airport / Jodhpur Airport
Tour Type: Private Wildlife Photography Tour
Ideal For: Wildlife photographers, birders, nature lovers, documentary travelers

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Jaipur

Arrive in Jaipur and transfer to hotel.

  • Tour briefing & photography discussion
  • Evening local market walk (optional)
  • Overnight stay in Jaipur

Day 2: Jaipur to Ranthambore

Drive to Ranthambore National Park.

  • Check-in at wildlife resort
  • Evening nature photography session
  • Wildlife orientation

Overnight stay.

Day 3: Ranthambore Tiger Photography

Morning and evening jungle safaris.

Photography opportunities:

  • Royal Bengal Tigers
  • Leopards
  • Crocodiles
  • Birds & jungle landscapes

Ideal for:

  • Action photography
  • Safari portrait shots
  • Sunrise forest scenes

Day 4: Ranthambore to Bharatpur

Drive to Keoladeo National Park.

  • Sunset bird photography session
  • Wetland landscape photography

Overnight stay in Bharatpur.

Day 5: Full-Day Bird Photography

Explore Bharatpur sanctuary with expert naturalists.

Possible sightings:

  • Painted Storks
  • Pelicans
  • Owls
  • Kingfishers
  • Migratory birds

Activities:

  • Rickshaw birding tour
  • Seasonal boat ride
  • Macro & telephoto photography opportunities

Day 6: Bharatpur to Jawai

Travel towards Jawai Leopard Hills.

Known for:

  • Wild leopards
  • Granite hill landscapes
  • Rabari tribal culture

Evening leopard safari.

Day 7: Jawai Leopard Safari

Morning & evening photography safari.

Highlights:

  • Leopard tracking
  • Shepherd lifestyle photography
  • Rocky terrain landscapes
  • Birdlife around Jawai Dam

Overnight stay in luxury camp / resort.

Day 8: Jawai to Jaisalmer

Drive to Jaisalmer.

  • Sunset desert photography
  • Sand dune landscape shots

Optional:

  • Camel portrait photography
  • Night sky photography

Day 9: Desert National Park Photography

Visit Desert National Park.

Possible sightings:

  • Great Indian Bustard
  • Desert fox
  • Eagles & vultures
  • Sandgrouse

Golden hour photography in the Thar Desert.

Evening cultural desert camp experience.

Day 10: Departure from Jodhpur / Jaisalmer

After breakfast, transfer for departure.

 

Tour ends with unforgettable wildlife photography memories of Rajasthan.
