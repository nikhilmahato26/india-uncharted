Experience India’s most iconic heritage circuit combined with spirituality and ancient temple architecture on this Golden Triangle Tour with Khajuraho and Varanasi. This journey takes you through the royal cities of Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur, the UNESCO temples of Khajuraho, and the sacred ghats of Varanasi.

This tour is ideal for travelers who want to explore India’s history, culture, spirituality, architecture, and local traditions in one unforgettable trip.

Tour Overview

Duration: 9 Nights / 10 Days
Destinations Covered: Delhi – Agra – Jaipur – Khajuraho – Varanasi
Pickup & Drop: Delhi Airport / Varanasi Airport
Tour Type: Private Heritage & Cultural Tour
Ideal For: International travelers, cultural explorers, photographers, families, couples

Tour Itinerary

 

Day 1: Arrival in Delhi

Welcome to New Delhi.

After arrival:

  • Meet your private driver
  • Transfer to hotel
  • Relaxation after journey

Optional:

  • Evening market walk or food tour

Overnight stay in Delhi.

Day 2: Delhi Sightseeing

Explore the historical attractions of Delhi.

Visit:

  • India Gate
  • Qutub Minar
  • Humayun's Tomb
  • Lotus Temple

Drive past:

  • Parliament House
  • Rashtrapati Bhavan

Overnight stay.

Day 3: Delhi to Agra

Drive to Agra.

Visit:

  • Agra Fort

Optional:

  • Sunset at Mehtab Bagh

Overnight stay in Agra.

Day 4: Taj Mahal Sunrise – Jaipur Transfer

Early morning visit to:

  • Taj Mahal

Enjoy sunrise photography and explore the monument.

Later drive to Jaipur.

En route visit:

  • Fatehpur Sikri

Overnight stay in Jaipur.

Day 5: Jaipur Heritage Tour

Explore Jaipur’s royal attractions.

Visit:

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar

Evening:

  • Shopping in local bazaars
  • Traditional Rajasthani dinner (optional)

Day 6: Jaipur to Khajuraho

Travel towards Khajuraho by flight/train connection.

Relax at hotel after arrival.

Overnight stay.

Day 7: Khajuraho Temple Tour

Explore the world-famous:

  • Khajuraho Group of Monuments

Highlights:

  • UNESCO World Heritage temples
  • Ancient Hindu & Jain architecture
  • Detailed stone carvings
  • Sunrise & sunset photography opportunities

Optional:

  • Light & sound show in evening

Overnight stay.

Day 8: Khajuraho to Varanasi

Travel to Varanasi.

Evening experience:

  • Attend the famous Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat

Witness spiritual rituals, lamps, and chanting by the Ganges River.

Overnight stay.

Day 9: Varanasi Spiritual Tour

Early morning boat ride on the River Ganges.

Visit:

  • Ancient ghats of Varanasi
  • Local temples & narrow old streets
  • Spiritual & cultural landmarks

Optional:

  • Sarnath excursion

Photography opportunities:

  • Sunrise boat photography
  • Ritual & street photography

Overnight stay.

Day 10: Departure from Varanasi

After breakfast, transfer to airport for departure.

Tour ends with unforgettable memories of India’s heritage, spirituality, and architecture.
