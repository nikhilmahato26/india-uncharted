Why Visit Bharatpur?

Bharatpur is one of Rajasthan’s hidden gems, famous for its incredible birdlife and historical attractions. The city is home to the renowned Keoladeo National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the finest bird sanctuaries in the world. Every year, thousands of migratory birds from across the globe visit the park, making it a paradise for bird watchers and wildlife photographers.

Apart from its natural beauty, Bharatpur also offers rich royal heritage and cultural experiences. Travelers can explore the historic Lohagarh Fort, known for its strong architecture and fascinating history. The city’s peaceful environment, scenic landscapes, local markets, and traditional Rajasthani culture make it an ideal destination for a relaxing holiday.

Bharatpur is also conveniently located near Agra, Jaipur, and Delhi, making it a popular stop on the Golden Triangle route. Visitors can enjoy bird safaris, cycle rickshaw rides inside the sanctuary, heritage sightseeing, local food experiences, and nature walks during their trip.
