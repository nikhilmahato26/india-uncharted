Why Visit Agra?

Agra is one of India’s most iconic travel destinations and a major part of the famous Golden Triangle circuit. The city is known for its rich Mughal heritage, impressive forts, ancient architecture, and delicious street food. From sunrise views of the Taj Mahal to exploring grand palaces and bustling bazaars, Agra offers a perfect mix of history and culture for every traveler.
