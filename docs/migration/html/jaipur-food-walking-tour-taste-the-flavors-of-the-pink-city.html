Tour Itinerary

🚶 Start the Food Walk in Old Jaipur

Begin your tour in the historic streets of:

  • Johari Bazaar
  • Bapu Bazaar
  • Chandpole Market

Walk through colorful lanes filled with local aromas, spice shops, sweets, and traditional cafés.

🥟 Taste Famous Jaipur Street Food

🍴 Pyaaz Kachori

Try Jaipur’s iconic spicy onion-filled kachori served with chutneys.

🍛 Dal Kachori & Samosa

Enjoy crispy Rajasthani snacks loved by locals.

🥛 Traditional Lassi

Taste thick creamy lassi served in traditional kulhad cups.

Perfect refreshment during the walking tour.

🍬 Rajasthani Sweets

Taste famous sweets like:

  • Ghewar
  • Mawa Kachori
  • Rabri
  • Gulab Jamun

Learn about festive dessert traditions of Rajasthan.

🌶️ Chaat & Local Snacks

Enjoy:

  • Aloo tikki chaat
  • Mirchi bada
  • Masala chai
  • Street-side snacks
