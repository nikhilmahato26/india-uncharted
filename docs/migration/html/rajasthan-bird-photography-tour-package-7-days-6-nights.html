Rajasthan Bird Photography Tour Package (7 Days / 6 Nights)

If you’re passionate about bird photography and wildlife, this Rajasthan Bird Photography Tour is designed to give you the best opportunities for capturing migratory birds, wetlands, desert species, and rare wildlife in natural habitats. The itinerary is planned around ideal photography timings with enough flexibility for serious photographers.

Tour Overview

Duration: 6 Nights / 7 Days
Destinations Covered: Bharatpur – Tal Chhapar – Khichan – Jaisalmer
Pickup & Drop: Jaipur / Jodhpur Airport or Railway Station
Tour Type: Wildlife & Bird Photography Tour
Ideal For: Bird photographers, wildlife enthusiasts, nature travelers

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Jaipur – Transfer to Bharatpur

After arrival, drive to Keoladeo National Park, one of India’s most famous bird sanctuaries.

  • Check-in at nature resort
  • Evening bird photography session
  • Sunset wetland photography

Overnight stay in Bharatpur.

Day 2: Full-Day Bird Photography at Bharatpur

Early morning photography session inside the sanctuary.

Possible sightings:

  • Painted Storks
  • Pelicans
  • Kingfishers
  • Owls
  • Herons & migratory ducks

Activities:

  • Rickshaw-guided birding tour
  • Optional boat ride (seasonal)
  • Golden hour photography

Overnight stay.

Day 3: Bharatpur to Tal Chhapar

Drive towards Tal Chhapar Sanctuary.

Known for:

  • Raptors
  • Blackbucks
  • Desert birds

Evening photography session during sunset.

Overnight stay near sanctuary.

Day 4: Tal Chhapar Birding Experience

Morning safari & photography session.

Highlights:

  • Harriers
  • Eagles
  • Falcons
  • Demoiselle cranes (seasonal)

Afternoon free for editing & rest.

Day 5: Tal Chhapar to Khichan & Jaisalmer

Visit Khichan Bird Feeding Point, famous for thousands of migratory Demoiselle Cranes.

Photography opportunities:

  • Crane feeding scenes
  • Flight photography
  • Village wildlife interaction

Continue to Jaisalmer.

Day 6: Desert Bird Photography in Jaisalmer

Visit Desert National Park.

Possible sightings:

  • Great Indian Bustard
  • Desert Fox
  • Eagles & vultures
  • Sandgrouse

Sunset photography in desert landscapes.

Overnight stay in desert camp or hotel.

Day 7: Departure

 

After breakfast, transfer for departure from Jaisalmer / Jodhpur.
