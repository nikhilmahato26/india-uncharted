Why Visit Amritsar?

Amritsar is one of India’s most important spiritual and historical destinations, attracting visitors from around the world. The city is best known for the magnificent Golden Temple, the holiest shrine of Sikhism, where travelers can experience peace, devotion, and community service through the world’s largest free kitchen (Langar).

Apart from its spiritual significance, Amritsar is also famous for its rich history and Punjabi culture. Travelers can visit the historic Jallianwala Bagh, witness the patriotic Wagah Border ceremony, explore colorful local bazaars, and enjoy authentic Punjabi food including Amritsari Kulcha and Lassi.

The city offers a perfect mix of spirituality, heritage, shopping, and local experiences for every traveler. From peaceful morning prayers at the Golden Temple to vibrant evening markets and cultural performances, Amritsar promises unforgettable memories.
