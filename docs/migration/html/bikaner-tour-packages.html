Why Visit Bikaner?

Bikaner is one of Rajasthan’s most fascinating heritage cities, known for its rich royal history and stunning desert landscapes. The city is home to the magnificent Junagarh Fort, a beautiful fort famous for its impressive architecture, royal palaces, courtyards, and museums that reflect the grandeur of Rajasthan’s royal past.

Travelers can also explore the unique Karni Mata Temple, popularly known as the Rat Temple, which attracts visitors from around the world because of its spiritual significance and unusual traditions. Bikaner is also famous for camel breeding farms, vibrant bazaars, traditional handicrafts, and authentic Rajasthani snacks such as Bikaneri Bhujia.

The city offers visitors an unforgettable desert experience with camel safaris, cultural performances, folk music, and scenic sunsets over the sand dunes. From exploring royal heritage sites to enjoying local cuisine and colorful markets, Bikaner provides a perfect cultural experience for every traveler.
