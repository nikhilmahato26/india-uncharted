Kashmir Paradise Tour Package (5 Days / 4 Nights)

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Kashmir with this Kashmir Paradise Tour Package covering Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam. From peaceful lakes and snow-covered mountains to scenic valleys and traditional houseboat stays, this tour offers the perfect blend of relaxation, nature, adventure, and Kashmiri culture.

This package is ideal for honeymoon couples, families, nature lovers, photographers, and travelers looking for a memorable Himalayan holiday.

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Srinagar – Houseboat Experience

Welcome to Srinagar.

After airport pickup:

  • Transfer to traditional houseboat on Dal Lake
  • Relax amidst peaceful mountain surroundings

Evening:

  • Enjoy Shikara ride on Dal Lake
  • Explore floating markets & lakeside views

Dinner & overnight stay in houseboat.

Day 2: Srinagar Local Sightseeing

Explore Srinagar’s famous attractions.

Visit:

  • Mughal Gardens
  • Nishat Garden
  • Shalimar Garden
  • Chashme Shahi

Optional:

  • Local Kashmiri handicraft shopping
  • Traditional Kashmiri cuisine experience

Overnight stay in Srinagar hotel.

Day 3: Gulmarg Excursion

Drive to Gulmarg, one of India’s most famous hill stations.

Activities:

  • Gondola cable car ride (optional)
  • Snow activities during winter
  • Skiing & mountain photography

Enjoy panoramic Himalayan views.

Return to Srinagar or overnight in Gulmarg depending on package selection.

Day 4: Pahalgam Valley Tour

Drive to Pahalgam.

En route:

  • Saffron fields
  • Apple orchards
  • Scenic mountain villages

Visit:

  • Betaab Valley
  • Aru Valley (optional)
  • Lidder River viewpoints

Enjoy peaceful nature surroundings.

Overnight stay.

Day 5: Departure from Srinagar

After breakfast, transfer to Srinagar Airport for departure.

Tour ends with unforgettable memories of Kashmir’s mountains, lakes, and valleys.
